# HopeForScope 🧭
> **"Your Future. Your Choices. Your Scope."**

*"Your marks should help guide your future, not limit your future. Every student deserves to know their opportunities, understand their choices, and discover their scope."*

---

## 🌟 Overview

**HopeForScope** is a modern, intelligent, and student-friendly career guidance platform built to empower students after Class 10, Class 12, graduation, and diploma programs.

It acts as a compassionate personal career counsellor, offering personalized educational roadmaps, degree courses, entrance examinations, verified coaching institutes, study abroad guidance, and emerging future-oriented technology careers.

## 🚀 Key Modules & Capabilities

### 1. Career Discovery After Class 10
- **13-Question Conversational Assessment:** Asks questions one at a time (Name, Location, Marks, Board, Favourite Subjects, Highest Marks Subjects, Difficult Subjects, Interests, Stream Preference, Career Domain, Study Destination, Budget, Learning Mode).
- **Stream Recommendations:** Evaluates Science (PCM, PCB, PCMB), Commerce (with & without Math), Arts & Humanities, Vocational Education, and Polytechnic Diplomas with compatibility scores, subject lists, entrance exams, and costs.
- **Personalized Career Roadmap:** Step-by-step visual journey: Class 10 → Class 12 → Entrance Exams → Degrees → Skills → Internships → First Job → 10-Year Career Growth.

### 2. Career Discovery After Class 12
- Analyzes stream, individual marks, strongest/weakest subjects, budget, and time horizon.
- Recommends Degree courses (B.Tech, MBBS, B.Des, Law), fast-track diplomas, CA, emerging tech specializations, and government pathways.
- For each course: Duration, eligibility, minimum %, entrance exams, admission process, top colleges, fees, career options, salary ranges (India & abroad), and future demand.

### 3. Explore Every Career in Detail
- 25+ deeply detailed profiles covering Technology, Healthcare, Commerce & Finance, Law, Design, Aviation, and Governance.
- Daily responsibilities, work environment, certifications (CEH, OSCP, AWS, CFA, RCI, DGCA), salary ranges (Entry, Mid, Senior in India and globally), advantages, challenges, and alternative paths.

### 4. FutureScope – Careers of Tomorrow
- Dedicated hub covering Ethical Hacking, AI & ML, Blockchain, EV Technology, Robotics & Drones, Quantum Computing, Bioinformatics, and Clean Energy.
- Categorized by readiness: **Established**, **Emerging**, and **Speculative Future**.

### 5. Entrance Exams Database
- Searchable database across Engineering (JEE Main, Advanced, BITSAT), Medical (NEET-UG), Commerce/Management (CUET, CA Foundation, CLAT, IPMAT), Government/Defence (UPSC, NDA, CDS, SSC, Bank PO), and International (IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, GRE).
- Syllabus highlights, exam patterns, eligibility, important dates, and official registration portal links.

### 6. GlobalScope – Study Beyond Borders
- Detailed guides for **Germany** (tuition-free public universities, 18-month job seeker visa), **USA** (3-year STEM OPT), **UK** (1-year Master's, Graduate Route), **Canada**, **Australia**, and **Ireland**.
- Tuition fees in INR and local currency, living expenses, blocked accounts, part-time work rights, post-study work authorization, and PR pathways.

### 7. Find Coaching Institutes Near Me
- Location-based finder across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Kota, and Hyderabad.
- Filter by exam (JEE, NEET, UPSC, MHT-CET, IELTS, Cybersecurity), distance, verified fees, ratings, and teaching mode (Offline, Online, Hybrid).

### 8. Career Comparison Tool
- Side-by-side comparison of 2 or 3 careers (e.g. *Cybersecurity vs Software Dev*, *CA vs Investment Banker*, *Doctor vs Bioinformatics*, *Corporate Lawyer vs IAS Officer*).
- AI Personalized Match Verdict analyzing compatibility based on individual preferences.

### 9. Job and Government Career Guidance
- Clear qualifications, age limits, competitive exams, selection stages, pay bands, and official application portals for government jobs, private IT, research (ISRO/DRDO), freelancing, and startups.

### 10. Scholarships & Financial Assistance
- Central Sector Scheme (NSP/PM-USP), DST INSPIRE, Reliance Foundation, AICTE Pragati, DAAD Germany, Chevening UK, and Vidya Lakshmi education loan schemes.

### 11. AI Career Counsellor (ScopeBot)
- Supportive, compassionate assistant answering questions like:
  - *"I scored 65% in Class 10. What can I do?"*
  - *"I am confused between Commerce and Science."*
  - *"Can I enter cybersecurity after studying Commerce?"*
  - *"Which careers are possible without mathematics?"*
  - *"How can I study in Germany with low tuition fees?"*

### 12. Personalized Student Dashboard & Admin Hub
- Track profile, save careers, colleges, exams, study milestones, action checklist, and export printable Career Guidance Reports.
- Admin view with live query monitoring, analytics, and data source verification logs.

## 💻 How to Open and Run

### Option 1: Direct Browser Launch
Open `index.html` directly in your favorite web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari):
```
file:///c:/Users/dell/Downloads/New folder/.vscode/hope-for-scope/index.html
```

### Option 2: Local PowerShell Web Server
In PowerShell, navigate to the directory and run:
```powershell
cd "c:\Users\dell\Downloads\New folder\.vscode\hope-for-scope"
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\serve.ps1 -Port 5000
```
Then open `http://localhost:5000` in your browser.
